Reusable teaching skill · version 1.0 · 5 October 2026

Explain a harmonic oscillator through documented Feynman methods.

A portable workflow for a classical linear oscillator, grounded in Caltech's edited lecture text. It records what supports each method, where the evidence stops, and how to evaluate an explanation. Intended for mathematically literate learners; no imitation of Feynman's voice is required.

Checked: skill format, relative references and seven mathematical fixture groups pass. Unmeasured: behavioral performance, learner benefit, added value and fidelity to Feynman.

Use the skill

Follow the progression: Nate's Karpathy tutorial → general builder guide → Feynman construction tutorial → worked oscillator lesson.

The bundle is local and has not been installed globally. From the project root, give an agent this instruction:

Read ./skills/feynman-harmonic-oscillator/SKILL.md and its evidence reference.
Use that workflow to explain a spring oscillator with m = 2 kg,
k = 18 N/m, x(0) = 0.3 m and v(0) = -0.6 m/s.
Include the initial-condition and energy checks, source locators,
and one question that tests transfer to a changed case.

The complete folder can be copied into a supported skills directory; after discovery, invoke $feynman-harmonic-oscillator. Keep the evidence and evaluation files with the entrypoint.

Chapters share one course/book lineage; they are not independent confirmation of universal habits. The text credits Feynman, Leighton and Sands, and is edited rather than a verbatim record.

What the evidence leaves open

  • No original audio or figure inspection, learner outcome study, or fresh expert reference answers.
  • No support for a universal four-step “Feynman technique,” compulsory child-level language, or quantum/nonlinear expertise from these sources alone.
  • The skill records a mismatch in the discussion of undamped transients, and conditions the correction on damping. It distinguishes mathematical reasoning from expert attribution.
  • Exact lecture dates, individual sentence authorship and online revision identifiers were not verified.

The highest-value next step is a fresh learner transfer case under controlled baseline conditions. That can test usefulness without arranging interviews or contacting the expert.

Access and portability

This bundle contains original synthesis, source links and authored fixtures, with no raw lecture corpus or copied figures. The public edition remains copyrighted; public access does not establish redistribution permission. Preserve the ledger's rights notes. The package has relative internal references and no private machine paths.

The original skill construction was local, without interviews, agents, hooks or global installation. The user subsequently authorized this derived package and its tutorials for publication on GitHub Pages. Behavioral comparisons and expert fidelity remain unmeasured.
